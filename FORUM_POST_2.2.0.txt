## 🎉 Update: Topic Rating 2.2.0 + Thumb Sliders 2.1.0

A coordinated update for the TryHackX discussion-list stack — new ways to show ratings on the list, a clickable single star, finer per-tag control, and a shared avatar setting that both extensions keep in sync. 🙌

### ⭐ Topic Rating 2.2.0

**Pick how the rating looks on the discussion list — separately for desktop and mobile.** Six styles:

- **Full stars — in place** (the classic look, above views/replies — still the default)
- **One star + score**, e.g. `★4.6` **— in place**
- **One graded star** (empty / half / full by score) **+ score — in place**
- …and the same three **appended to the end of the title**

The graded star uses the 0–10 scale: empty `0–3.33`, half `3.33–6.67`, full `>6.67` (10 = 5.0).

**Clickable single star + rate from the modal.** When you choose a single-star style, two new toggles appear:

- *Make the single star clickable* — the whole star + number opens the ratings list in a modal (even after the title).
- *Allow rating inside the modal* — adds an interactive rating control at the top of that modal, so people can rate without leaving the list.

**Finer per-tag control (when Tags is enabled):**

- **Secondary tags used on their own** — a new list controlling discussions tagged with *only a secondary tag and no primary* (e.g. announcements posted bypassing tag-count limits). Each is Enabled/Disabled, **default Disabled** (opt-in). This fixes a case where such topics were always rateable regardless of your settings.
- **Allow rating on discussions with no tags** — switch tagless discussions off entirely.
- **Hide ratings on rating-disabled topics** *(on by default)* — if a topic becomes non-rateable because all its tags are disabled (e.g. its tag was switched to a disabled one after it was rated), the existing ratings are hidden instead of shown read-only. Prevents "rate it, then lock in the score" abuse. Users who *can* rate still see it.
- **Hide empty stars from users who can't rate** — on the list, don't show the empty widget to non-voters on topics with no ratings yet.

**Fixes:** the ratings modal title no longer reads `Ratings ({undefined})`; the modal separator is a softer `#3c3c41`; the in-modal rate control is centred; and the single empty star is properly coloured.

> ⚠️ **Heads-up:** discussions tagged with **only a secondary tag (no primary)** now default to **not** rateable (they were always rateable before). Re-enable the ones you want under *Secondary tags used on their own*.

### 🖼️ Thumb Sliders 2.1.0 — and a shared avatar control

The old *Replace avatar with thumbnail* setting is now a **shared, per-device avatar section** (Desktop / Mobile):

- **Show avatar**
- **Replace with thumbnail when the topic has an image**
- **Always replace with thumbnail**
- **Hide avatar** *(new)* — drop the author avatar entirely for a lighter list (great on mobile). Works even without thumbnails.

The same section appears in **both** Topic Rating and Thumb Sliders, and **changing it in one updates the other** — install either or both (*"together or separate"*). Your previous *Replace avatar* choice is migrated automatically.

### ⬆️ Updating

```bash
composer update tryhackx/flarum-topic-rating tryhackx/flarum-thumb-sliders
php flarum migrate
php flarum cache:clear
```

> `php flarum migrate` is required for Thumb Sliders 2.1.0 (it seeds the new shared avatar setting from your old one) — safe to run either way.

As always, feedback and bug reports are very welcome. Enjoy! 🚀
